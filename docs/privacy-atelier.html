Privacy

Mandala Atelier

Deutsch English

This app collects no data. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no connection to the internet. Whatever you make in the atelier stays on your device — we never get to see it.

What is stored

Everything this app stores stays on your device alone, in the storage belonging to the browser or the app. None of it is transmitted, analysed or backed up anywhere.

Your work Finished mandalas, with whatever title you give them. They sit in the gallery and stay there until you delete them.
People on this device So that several people can share one iPad without their galleries mixing, a name can be entered per person. The name is optional, freely chosen, and never leaves the device. Leaving the field empty is expressly allowed.
Your settings The motif last chosen, the colour world and any colours you mixed yourself, the number of axes, mirroring, tool, stroke width, guides, and light or dark.

Technically: the settings and the list of people in localStorage under the prefix mandala-atelier., the finished work in the IndexedDB store mandala-atelier.

What does not happen

  • No account, no sign-in, no registration.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifier (IDFA).
  • No analytics, no statistics, no crash reports.
  • Nothing passed to third parties — there is nothing to pass on.
  • No third-party services, no CDN, no fonts fetched at runtime. The fonts are embedded in the program as data.
  • No location, no contacts, no microphone, no camera.

At runtime the app fetches nothing from the network. That is not a statement of intent but a tested fact: the project's own test run counts every request that does not come from the program itself and fails the moment one appears.

When you pass something on yourself

Two paths lead out of the app, and you take both of them — the app does not:

  • Saving and sharing. You can pass a piece of work or a backup file on through your device's share sheet. Where it goes is your decision in that moment; from there the terms of whatever destination you chose apply.
  • Printing. The print sheet goes to your device's printing system. We see nothing of it.

How to delete everything

A single piece of work is deleted in the gallery. A person is deleted in the person list; their work goes with them.

All of it at once: delete the app from your device. If the atelier is used as a web app in a browser, clearing the website data removes everything just the same. There is no need to withdraw consent or to ask us — and it would have no effect, because nothing of yours is held by us.

Your rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and data portability. Those rights are held against a body that processes your data. We process none. Any information we could give you would necessarily be empty.

What sits on your device is in your hands anyway: look at it, change it, export it as a backup file, delete it — all inside the app, without asking us.

Right to complain. Independently of that, you may lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority at any time. The competent one is enter competent supervisory authority — which one it is depends on where the provider is based, and will be settled together with that.

Children

The app is not aimed at any particular age and collects nothing from anyone — children included.

Changes

If anything in the app changes that touches this page, this page changes with it. What applies is the version standing here at the time you use the app.
