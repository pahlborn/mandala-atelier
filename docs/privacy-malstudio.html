This app collects no data. No account, no advertising, no analytics. Your child's first name, their pictures and everything you leave behind for the treasure hunt stay on the iPad — we never get to see any of it.

What is stored

Everything this app stores stays on your device alone. None of it is transmitted, analysed or backed up anywhere — the first name included.

The child's profile The first name you or your child enter, and the animal chosen. The name is freely chosen; a nickname does just as well. Several children get several profiles.
The pictures Everything in the gallery, along with a note of which motifs have already been painted.
Progress Stars, medals, stickers, the rank from Beach Child up to Ocean Child, and how far the treasure island has been uncovered.
What you leave for the treasure hunt The hiding place as coordinates, your hint text and your photo of the spot. You enter all three voluntarily in the parents' area, all three stay on the device, and all three can be deleted there individually.
Settings Language, reading voice, sound on or off, the mode last used, and the two releases from the parents' area.

About the photo of the hiding place. It is kept only in the app's own storage and shown only there. It does not travel into your photo library, into any cloud, or anywhere else. What a photo otherwise carries with it — where and when it was taken — thus does not leave the device either.

What does not happen

  • No account, no sign-in, no registration.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifier (IDFA).
  • No analytics, no statistics, no crash reports.
  • No chat, no messages, no connection to other users.
  • No in-app purchases and no subscription.
  • Nothing passed to third parties — there is nothing to pass on.
  • No access to location. You enter the hiding place; the app does not ask the device where it is.
  • No microphone. The music and the sound of the sea are generated inside the device.

The two ways out

The app runs entirely without a network. There are exactly two places where a tap leads out of it — and both are closed as shipped. They open only if you expressly release them in the parents' area, and the only way in there is an arithmetic question that a child of the intended age will not solve.

  • Wikipedia. The Albarella knowledge section offers fifteen topics to read further, in German, English or Italian depending on the language set. With the release switched off, those buttons do not appear at all.
  • Apple Maps. If you have left a hiding place, a button in the treasure chest leads there. All that is handed over is the coordinate you entered yourself — nothing else: no name, no hint text, no photo.

What happens after that rests with Apple, or with Wikipedia. Their terms apply, not ours; we learn nothing of the call.

One exception, for honesty's sake. Anyone who used the signpost before version 7-52 — that is, who had already left a hiding place — keeps the map button open. Switching it off silently would have been a step backwards that nobody asked for. It can be closed at any time in the parents' area. Wikipedia is not covered by this exception and starts closed in every case.

The reading voice

The app reads every step aloud. To do so it hands the text to your device's speech function and uses the voices installed there. On Apple devices the speaking happens on the device. We receive neither the text nor a recording; nothing is listened in on and nothing is recorded.

Pictures and fonts in the app

The photographs of the island come from Wikimedia Commons and are under free licences (CC BY, CC BY-SA, CC0, public domain); author and licence are shown on each picture. They ship with the app at reduced size and are not fetched at runtime. The fonts are embedded in the program as data. Both mean the same thing: no connection to the outside arises for them either.

How to delete everything

A single picture is deleted in the gallery. A profile and everything belonging to it is deleted on the start screen. Hiding place, hint and photo are deleted individually in the parents' area; the treasure hunt can also be reset there as a whole.

All of it at once: delete the app from your device. If the studio is used as a web app in a browser, clearing the website data removes everything just the same. There is no need to ask us, and it would have no effect — nothing of yours is held by us.

Your rights

Access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, data portability: these rights under the General Data Protection Regulation are held against a body that processes your data. We process none. Any information we could give you would necessarily be empty. What sits on the iPad is in your hands anyway.

Right to complain. Independently of that, you may lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority at any time. The competent one is enter competent supervisory authority — which one it is depends on where the provider is based, and will be settled together with that.

Children

The app is made for children from about five years old. That is precisely why what stands above matters here most of all: nothing is collected from a child and nothing is sent. There is no advertising, no analytics, and nobody a child could get in touch with.

The first name a child enters serves only to tell their pictures apart from their siblings'. It is shown on the device and nowhere else. Anyone who would rather not write it uses a nickname — the app checks nothing.

The two ways out sit behind the parents' gate for the same reason: a child should not be able to leave the app by accident.

Changes

If anything in the app changes that touches this page — a new way out above all — this page changes with it. What applies is the version standing here at the time you use the app.
