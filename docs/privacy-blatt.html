Mandala – The Quiet Leaf

Deutsch English

This app collects no data. No account, no advertising, no analytics, no connection to the internet. The app does not even ask you for a name.

What is stored

Everything stays on your device. Nothing is transmitted, analysed or backed up anywhere.

Your sheets The pile of sheets you have started and set aside, along with whatever you have rubbed out of them. A sheet does not remember the picture but the few figures it can be rebuilt from — seed, world, character and grammar.
Three small things Whether sound is on or off; whether you have seen the introduction; which of the quiet hints have already been shown to you. The app puts down nothing else.

Technically: the three small things in localStorage under atelier3-ton, atelier3-gesehen and atelier3-gedanken, the sheets in the app's IndexedDB store.

No name, no person, no profile. Unlike Mandala Atelier there is no person management here. The app does not know who you are, and does not ask.

What does not happen

  • No account, no sign-in, no registration.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifier (IDFA).
  • No analytics, no statistics, no crash reports.
  • Nothing passed to third parties — there is nothing to pass on.
  • No third-party services, no CDN, no fonts fetched at runtime.
  • No location, no contacts, no microphone, no camera.

About the sound: rubbing makes a sound, and that sound is generated inside the device — filtered noise that follows the speed of your hand. Nothing is recorded, and nothing is played that came from outside. The microphone stays untouched.

At runtime the app fetches nothing from the network. The project's own test run checks this with a line of its own and fails the moment something is fetched from outside after all.

How to delete everything

A single sheet is set aside or discarded in the pile. All of it at once: delete the app from your device. If the app is used in a browser, clearing the website data removes everything just the same. There is no need to ask us, and it would have no effect — nothing of yours is held by us.

Your rights

Access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, data portability: these rights under the General Data Protection Regulation are held against a body that processes your data. We process none. Any information we could give you would necessarily be empty.

Right to complain. Independently of that, you may lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority at any time. The competent one is enter competent supervisory authority — which one it is depends on where the provider is based, and will be settled together with that.

Children

The app collects nothing from anyone — children included.

Changes

If anything in the app changes that touches this page, this page changes with it. What applies is the version standing here at the time you use the app.
